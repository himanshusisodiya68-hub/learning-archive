Your subjects
How this archive helps you remember
Recall before readingEvery lesson opens with a question, and checks sit between sections. Pulling an answer from memory strengthens it more than rereading does.
Spaced reviewsCards and missed problems come back at growing intervals, just before you would forget them.
Mixed practiceDaily sets shuffle topics and levels, so you learn to choose the method, not just apply it.
Words and picturesEach idea comes with a diagram or simulation as well as text, giving memory two routes in.
Your own wordsThe notes tab asks you to explain each idea simply, which shows exactly where your understanding has gaps.
